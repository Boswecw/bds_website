Behind the Product

The BDS engineering foundation.

AuthorForge is the public product. ForgeCommand and BDS Core are the internal, governed engineering foundation that make its reliability possible.

State

Local-First Canonical State

Primary state belongs on your machine, not in a remote vendor silo. Cloud services remain optional support lanes for synchronization, backup, and explicitly requested capabilities.

Authority

Fail-Closed Capability Gateways

Models receive no ambient execution authority. Tool use, code mutation, and agent runs are bounded by declared capabilities, sandboxed execution, verification, and human authorization.

Inference

Hybrid Model Orchestration

Routine classification, state tracking, and fast reasoning stay local when practical. Higher-order synthesis escalates only through deliberate, governed routes.

Human authority is the root of trust. The infrastructure supports the work. It does not inherit creative or business authority.
